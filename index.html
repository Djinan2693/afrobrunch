Our Story

Every Flavor Tells a Story

Afro Brunch started with a simple frustration: you can find almost every cuisine in Metro Manila, except the one we grew up with. So we built an afternoon around it. Recipes from West and Central Africa, cooked the way they are cooked at home — slowly, generously, and for far too many people.

It is not a restaurant. It is one long table, once, on September 27. You come hungry, you sit with strangers, the afrobeats never stop, and you leave having eaten far more than you planned to.

On The Buffet

The Feast

  • Attiéké & Braised Beef

    Included

    Cassava couscous under slow-braised beef, grilled plantain and pepper skewers straight off the coals.

  • Grilled Tilapia & Attiéké

    Signature Included

    Whole tilapia grilled over charcoal, buried under a fresh pepper, corn and tomato salsa.

  • Kedjenou

    Included

    Chicken and beef sealed in a pot with sweet peppers and herbs, cooked in nothing but their own steam.

  • The Full Platter

    Crowd Favourite Included

    Cabbage rolls, carrot, cassava, okra and three meats piled on a bed of spiced attiéké.

  • Pepper Sauce & Meatballs

    Included

    A deep, glossy pepper stew with meatballs, scotch bonnet and rosemary. The one that empties first.

  • Puff Puff

    Included

    Warm sweet dough balls, golden and glazed, served all afternoon with ice-cold hibiscus.
